# Give August

> Giving Back. Bringing People Together. Making a Difference.

## 1. Homepage Introduction

### Because Every Life Matters.

We believe that making a difference starts with caring about the people around us.

Give August is our annual community outreach initiative, built on the belief that we all have a responsibility to make our communities better. It is an opportunity to step beyond our everyday activities, come together and extend a helping hand to those who need it.

Every community has people with stories, dreams, challenges and hopes for a better tomorrow. Through Give August, we create time to connect with these communities, understand their needs and offer support in ways that make a meaningful difference.

For us, giving back is not simply about what we give. It is about the relationships we build, the smiles we share and the lives we touch along the way.

We believe that even the smallest act of kindness can mean something to someone, and that together, we can do so much more.

Give August is our way of showing up, giving back and making a difference, one community at a time.

## 2. About Give August

### A Simple Idea. A Meaningful Purpose.

Give August was created around a simple but important belief: our responsibility to society goes beyond what we do in our everyday lives. We all have a part to play in making the world around us a better place.

Every year, we dedicate time, resources and effort towards reaching out to communities, supporting people and contributing to causes that matter.

We understand that behind every community are real people with real needs. There are children who deserve opportunities, families who need support and individuals who simply need to know that someone cares.

That is why Give August is important to us.

It gives us an opportunity to step closer to the people around us, listen to their experiences and offer support where we can. It is about treating people with dignity, sharing what we have and recognising that meaningful change begins when we care enough to take action.

Our journey began in 2025 with our first edition of Give August. In 2026, we returned for our second edition, building on the experience of the previous year and making an even greater impact.

Along the way, we have had the opportunity to support Good Samaritan Children's Home, an engagement that reflects the heart of what we stand for: caring for others, supporting those who need it and making people feel valued.

We know there is still so much more to do, but we remain committed to continuing this journey and finding more ways to make a positive difference.

Because giving back should never be just an event on the calendar. It should be part of who we are.

## 3. Our Mission and Vision

### Our Mission

To bring people together through compassion, generosity and meaningful community engagement. We strive to support those in need, respond to community challenges and contribute to a society where everyone has an opportunity to live a better life.

### Our Vision

A society where people care for one another, communities are supported and individuals and organisations work together to create a better future for generations to come.

## 4. Our Approach

### It Starts With People. It Grows Through Action.

We believe that meaningful change begins with understanding people. It means taking time to listen, recognising the needs within our communities and being willing to make a difference.

Our approach is guided by four simple values.

### 01. Understanding Our Communities

Before anything else, we believe in listening. Every community is different, and understanding people's experiences helps us recognise where support is needed most.

### 02. Giving With Compassion

Giving is more than providing material support. It is about the intention behind every contribution. Whether we give our time, resources, knowledge or skills, we do so with the hope of making someone's life a little better.

### 03. Building Meaningful Connections

We want our engagement with communities to go beyond a single day or activity. We value the relationships we build and believe that genuine connections are an important part of lasting impact.

### 04. Creating Change Together

We recognise that no single person or organisation can address every challenge. We believe in bringing people together, sharing responsibilities and working towards common goals.

## 5. Our Journey

### Every Edition, A Greater Reason to Give.

Give August is a growing journey. Each edition gives us another opportunity to reach people, strengthen connections and do more for the communities around us.

### 2025: The Beginning

Our first edition marked the beginning of our commitment to making community outreach an important part of what we do. It was an opportunity to put our belief in giving back into action and begin building meaningful relationships with the communities around us.

### 2026: Growing the Impact

Our second edition brought an even greater impact. Building on the foundation of our first year, we continued our outreach efforts, strengthened our commitment and reached more people through a shared spirit of giving and togetherness.

### A Community Close to Our Hearts

#### Good Samaritan Children's Home

One of the meaningful engagements in our Give August journey has been our support for Good Samaritan Children's Home.

We believe every child deserves to feel loved, valued and supported. Every child deserves the opportunity to grow, dream and look forward to a brighter future.

Supporting Good Samaritan Children's Home gave us an opportunity to put these beliefs into action and contribute to a cause that touches the lives of children.

For us, it is a reminder that sometimes the most meaningful thing we can do is show up, offer support and let others know that they matter.

We look forward to continuing to support communities and causes that make a difference in people's lives.

## 6. Creating Impact Together

### We Are Better When We Give Together.

We believe that making a lasting difference is a shared responsibility.

Across our communities, there are individuals and organisations working every day to improve lives, support vulnerable groups and create opportunities for others. We believe that bringing these efforts together can help us reach further and achieve more.

Give August is open to partnerships and collaborations with organisations, institutions, businesses and individuals who share our vision.

We welcome people who are willing to contribute their time, resources, skills, knowledge or services towards meaningful community initiatives.

### Who Can Be Part of Give August?

#### Corporate Organisations

Businesses interested in contributing to meaningful community outreach and making a positive social difference.

#### NGOs and Community-Based Organisations

Organisations with experience working directly with communities and supporting people in need.

#### Educational Institutions

Schools, colleges and universities interested in supporting education, children and opportunities for young people.

#### Healthcare Institutions and Providers

Healthcare professionals and organisations willing to contribute towards better community health and wellbeing.

#### Volunteers and Individuals

People who believe in giving back and are willing to share their time, talents, resources or expertise.

### Let's Work Together.

You do not have to do something extraordinary to make a difference. Sometimes, all it takes is a willingness to contribute and a genuine desire to help.

Whether you are looking to partner with us, volunteer, offer support or simply learn more about what we do, we would love to hear from you.

Together, we can reach more people, support more communities and create a greater impact.
