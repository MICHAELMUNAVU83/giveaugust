About Give August

A Simple Idea. A Meaningful Purpose.

Give August was created around a simple but important belief: our responsibility to society goes beyond what we do in our everyday lives. Every year, we dedicate time, resources and effort towards reaching out to communities and contributing to causes that matter.

Listen With Care

Act With Compassion

  • Treating people with dignity and recognising real needs.
  • Sharing our time, resources, knowledge and skills.
  • Building relationships that extend beyond a single day.

Our Story

From One Afternoon to a Movement.

Every August tells a chapter. Here is how a small team's decision to show up became a tradition that touches hundreds of lives.

  1. 1
    2025 · Where It Began

    A few of us, one afternoon

    Our first Give August was small: a handful of friends walking in with what we could carry. It was enough to know we had to come back, and bigger.

  2. 2
    2026 · Arriving

    Through the gates of Good Samaritan

    The truck was loaded with food and supplies. Then the gates opened, and we were welcomed in by the founder and the children who call this place home.

  3. 3
    2026 · Playtime

    Football, swings and a lot of laughter

    The plan was to help. The children had other ideas: a football match on the rooftop, swings, tunnels and peace signs for every camera.

  4. 4
    2026 · Together

    One family, for a day and beyond

    Lunch, cake and one big photo with everybody cheering. Every year we write a new page, and every one of them begins with someone deciding to show up.
